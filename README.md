🌍 Bharat Climate Twin AI

AI-Powered Digital Twin of India's Climate using National Datasets

Developed for Bharatiya Antariksh Hackathon 2026 (ISRO)


🚀 Overview

Bharat Climate Twin AI is an AI-powered Digital Twin platform designed to create a dynamic virtual representation of India's climate system using indigenous datasets from IMD and ISRO.

The platform integrates satellite observations, meteorological records, climate analytics, and machine learning models to generate high-resolution climate forecasts, risk intelligence, and scenario-based simulations.

By leveraging AI and geospatial technologies, Bharat Climate Twin AI empowers policymakers, researchers, disaster management authorities, and citizens with actionable climate intelligence.

🎯 Problem Statement

India faces increasing climate variability due to:

Heatwaves
Extreme rainfall
Floods
Droughts
Water scarcity
Agricultural uncertainty

Although large volumes of climate data are available through IMD and ISRO, these datasets often remain fragmented and difficult to convert into actionable insights.

There is a need for a unified, intelligent platform capable of:

Monitoring climate conditions
Predicting future climate patterns
Simulating climate scenarios
Supporting climate adaptation planning
